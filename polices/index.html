# VeloTV Privacy Policy **Last updated: October 28, 2026** ## 1. Introduction VeloTV is an IPTV player application that allows users to play media content provided through an external playlist server. This policy explains how information may be processed when using the application. ## 2. Information We Process Depending on the application version and configuration, the following information may be processed: - **MAC address and login credentials:** In versions where authentication is enabled, the application may use a MAC address and password to authenticate with the service provider's API. - **Playlist and media information:** The application connects to an external server to retrieve playlist information and media content, including channels, movies, and series. - **Technical connection information:** External servers may receive technical information such as IP addresses, connection timestamps, and request details as part of normal network operations. The current testing version may use a predefined MAC address and a fixed demo playlist instead of requiring users to log in. Information processing may differ between versions. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information may be processed to: - Retrieve and play playlist content. - Connect to the configured media or playlist server. - Authenticate users when login functionality is enabled. - Support the technical operation and security of server connections. ## 4. Third-Party Services VeloTV connects to external playlist and media servers to retrieve content. These services may process connection information or other data needed to provide their services. Their data handling practices are governed by their own policies. VeloTV currently does not integrate AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Sentry, or Crashlytics. ## 5. Data Sharing Information may be transmitted to the configured playlist server or service provider when necessary for content retrieval or authentication. External providers may independently process or retain information according to their own practices. ## 6. Data Retention and Security Data retention by external playlist or authentication providers depends on their respective practices. VeloTV cannot control the retention policies of independently operated third-party servers. Reasonable measures are taken within the application to handle network communications appropriately. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 7. Children's Privacy VeloTV is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you have concerns about information processed in connection with the application, please contact us. ## 8. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at the same URL, with the updated date shown above. ## 9.
